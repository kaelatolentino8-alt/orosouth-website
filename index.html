Geodetic & Land Titling Services

Delivering accurate boundary measurements, DENR/LRA documentation, and certified geodetic engineering expertise.

Land Titling

Complete assistance for Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT), judicial titling, and Registry of Deeds processing.

Land Documentation

Expert drafting of Legal Instruments, Extrajudicial Settlements, Deed of Absolute Sale, and BIR Tax Clearances.

Relocation Survey

Re-establishing exact missing boundary monuments (mojones) using high-precision GNSS/GPS equipment.

Verification Survey

Resolving land overlap disputes, encroachment issues, and title boundary verifications before purchase.

Original Survey

Initial land boundary surveying for alienable and disposable public land claims and DENR approvals.

Subdivision Survey

Partitioning mother titles into separate individual lot titles for family partitions or commercial selling.

Topographic Survey

Detailed elevation mapping, contour plotting, and terrain analysis for architectural & civil site development.

CAD Mapping & Plotting

Digital AutoCAD technical description plotting, blueprint drafting, and geospatial map layout creation.

Location Plan / Site Maps

Certified site plans required for municipal building permits, bank loan applications, and power grid connections.
